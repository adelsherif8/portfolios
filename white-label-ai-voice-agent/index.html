[05] Straight answers

What agencies
ask us first.

Will you ever contact my clients?

No. The agent is deployed inside your GoHighLevel agency account under your brand. We work with you, never with your client, and we will not pitch them anything. The relationship stays yours.

What does it cost me, and what do I charge?

$400 setup per account, then $250 a month per account with 500 call minutes included, and 15 cents a minute past that. Agencies typically resell it at $500 to $800 a month. You set the price and you send the invoice.

Is the first account really free?

Yes, on one condition: we get the before-and-after call numbers and permission to publish them anonymously. You pay in evidence instead of cash. It is limited to the first few partners.

What does the agent actually do?

It answers in about a second, qualifies the caller, books into the GoHighLevel calendar, writes the contact and notes into the CRM, and texts back missed calls. Anything urgent, angry or outside the rules goes to a human.

How fast is the second account?

The first build takes five to seven days. After that it is a reusable snapshot, so the next client in the same niche onboards in under an hour plus their own prices, hours and FAQs.
