[05] Straight answers

What plumbers
ask us first.

Will it quote a price it shouldn't?

No. It quotes the dispatch fee and any flat-rate items you give it, and nothing else. For anything that needs eyes on the job it says a tech will price it on site. It is not allowed to guess, and that rule sits above everything else in the script.

What about an actual emergency — a burst pipe at 2am?

It asks the safety questions first — is the water off, is there standing water, is anyone at risk — then either books the emergency slot or transfers straight to the on-call number, depending on the rules you set. Emergency handling is the one path we test hardest before launch.

Does it work with our existing number?

Yes. It sits on the number already on your vans and your Google profile, through LC Phone or your own Twilio account. Customers don't see a change.

Will customers know it's not a person?

It says it's an assistant if asked, and we recommend it says so up front. Most callers with a flooded kitchen care that someone is booking them today, not who typed the script. Pretending otherwise is how a business gets a bad review it can't answer.

How long until it's live?

Five to seven days from the call where you tell us your services, your prices, your service area and the three calls you miss most. Then it runs on real calls for thirty days with the baseline measured first, so you get a number rather than an impression.
