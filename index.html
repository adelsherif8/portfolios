    [08] Straight answers

    Including the ones that
    are fair objections.

    Will it sound like a robot?

    Open any agent above and judge for yourself — that's why they're the first thing on this page. The models are good enough now that most callers don't ask. What matters more is that it doesn't pretend: it says it's an assistant, and it hands over the moment the conversation needs a person.

    What happens when it can't answer something?

    It escalates. Every agent has explicit transfer rules — anything outside the service area, anything about a price it wasn't given, anything where the caller is upset. It takes the details, tells the caller a human will follow up, and notifies the owner immediately. It is never allowed to invent a price or promise a time.

    Does it work with our existing number and CRM?

    Yes. It sits on the number you already have, through LC Phone or your own Twilio account. GoHighLevel is the CRM we know deepest, but anything with an API works — the webhook layer is code, not a fixed integration.

    What does it cost to run each month?

    Usage runs roughly 8 to 16 cents per minute of conversation, billed to you at cost. A business taking 200 calls a month averaging two minutes lands around $30 to $65 in platform and telephony spend on top of the retainer. You'll have the real number for your call volume before you commit, not after.

    Can we put our own brand on it?

    That's the point of the agency tier. It deploys inside your GoHighLevel agency account, under your name, and We don't appear anywhere your client can see. We never contact your clients. The relationship is yours and so is the margin.

    How long before it's live?

    Five to seven days for the first agent, from the call where you tell us what it needs to handle. Every account after that is under an hour, because the snapshot carries the pipeline, calendars, fields and the agent with it.
