Visitors per day

Each bar is the number of different people who visited in that period. Hover a bar for page views too.

Top pages

Which pages get looked at most. Views count every load; visitors counts each person once.

Top blog posts

Same as top pages, but only blog articles.

Referrers

The website people clicked from to reach you (google.com, linkedin.com...). "Direct / none" means typed in, bookmarked, an email app, or the source hid itself.

UTM campaigns

Visits from links you tagged yourself, e.g. adelatya.com/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_campaign=post1. Use these on every link you share to see what works.

Countries

Where visitors are, from Vercel's IP lookup (the IP itself is never stored).

Devices

Phone, tablet or computer.

Entry pages

The first page each visitor landed on. This is your real front door, often a blog post rather than the home page.

Conversions and clicks

Actions that matter. conversion = sent the contact form, clicked the booking link, email or phone. Conversion rate = visitors who did at least one of those, divided by all visitors.

What exactly was clicked

Each click event with the button text or link it went to, and the page it happened on.

Live: last 30 minutes

Every page view and click in the last half hour, newest first. Refreshes every minute.

Google Search Console, 28 days

How the site shows up in Google search. Impressions = times a result from your site was shown. Clicks = times someone clicked it. CTR = clicks divided by impressions. Position = average rank in the results (1 is the top; 10 is the bottom of page one). Google reports with about a 2-day delay.
