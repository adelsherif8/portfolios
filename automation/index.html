[04] Questions

Asked before
every first workflow.

Do I need to pay for AI usage?

No. Automation workflows are rules and timers inside GoHighLevel and n8n. Your GoHighLevel subscription and SMS are billed to you by GoHighLevel as they are today. If a workflow reads documents with AI, that runs on our key and costs cents.

What is the care plan?

$200 a month, cancel any month: monitoring, fixes when GoHighLevel or a connected tool changes something, and up to two hours of changes a month — wording, day counts, new stages. You text, it's done. No re-quoting small things.

How fast is one workflow live?

Inside a week from getting access: a day to map it with you, two to build and test against your real scenarios, then you approve every message before it goes live.

Can it write to Asana, Sheets or QuickBooks?

Yes, through n8n. One outside integration is included in the $1,200 system; extra integrations are $400 each.

What do you need from me to start?

A user invite to your GoHighLevel sub-account, your stages in order, the messages you send today, who gets escalations, and what "stalled" means to you in days. That's the whole list.
