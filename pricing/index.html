Pricing questions

Asked before
every first invoice.

Why publish prices at all?

Because you are almost certainly comparing three options and two of them want a discovery call before they'll say a number. Publishing ours means you can rule us in or out in thirty seconds. If we're too expensive for what you need, we'd both rather know now.

Is the monthly optional?

For automation, yes — the $200 care plan is an add-on, and you can cancel it any month. For a voice agent, no: the monthly covers the call minutes, the number, monitoring and script changes, and an agent nobody maintains degrades within a quarter. We'd rather charge for that than pretend it doesn't happen.

Where does the money go on the voice monthly?

Roughly $50–60 of the $450 is hard cost — 500 minutes on the voice platform plus the phone number. The rest is the engineer who fixes it at 9pm, tunes it when you add a service, and reads the call logs so you don't have to.

What if it doesn't work?

We measure the baseline before launch — calls received, calls answered, jobs booked — so after thirty days there's a real comparison rather than an argument. If the numbers haven't moved, cancel the monthly. You keep the agent, the workflows and the data.

Is the first agency account really free?

Yes, on one condition: we get the before-and-after call numbers, and permission to publish them anonymously. You're paying in evidence instead of cash. That offer is limited to the first few partners, because the point of it is case studies, not volume.

Can I start with automation and add voice later?

That's the usual order. Most clients start with one $400 workflow, add the care plan when they want changes handled for them, and add the phone agent when they've seen what the follow-ups recover. The $1,500 bundle is for the ones who already know.
