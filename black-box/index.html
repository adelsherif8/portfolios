Design notes · teardown

A recorder, not a remote.

Layer 01 / 04

  1. 01

    The tape is the seek bar

    Two lanes drawn from the real audio: the AI agent above the line, the caller below. You see who talked, and for how long, before you press play.

    media-time-sliderbb-wavebb-flags

  2. 02

    Point anywhere, read the line

    Hovering the tape shows who is speaking at that moment and what they say. Flags mark the moments that matter: connected, booked, objection, closed.

    media-slider-previewbb-peekchapters.vtt

  3. 03

    Live instruments

    The level meters run off the actual audio and light the channel that is speaking. The REC lamp, the halt veil and the transcript are all player state.

    bb-metersbb-statusWebAudio

  4. 04

    Built to be used

    Every control is at least 44 px, works from the keyboard and keeps a visible focus ring. Text stays readable over bright and dark footage, and motion stops when reduced motion is on.
