[05] Straight answers

What owners
ask us first.

Can it take a whole order correctly?

It reads the menu you give it, repeats the order back before confirming, and sends the ticket through. Anything it isn't sure about — a substitution, an allergy — it hands to a person rather than guessing.

What about allergies?

It records what the guest says and flags it on the ticket, and it never tells a guest a dish is safe. Allergy questions are an explicit escalation in every restaurant agent we build.

Does it work with our POS?

It sits on your existing number and delivers the order the way you want it — to a tablet, a printer, an email, or straight into your system if it has an API.

Will guests hate it?

Some will, and it should never be the only option. Ours offers a human the moment somebody asks. The alternative you're comparing it to isn't a person, it's the phone ringing out.

How long until it's live?

Five to seven days, including your menu, your hours, your prep times and your escalation rules. Then thirty days live with the baseline measured first.
